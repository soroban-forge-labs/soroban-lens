# ADR-NNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem forced a decision? Include the constraints that mattered at the
time (scale, ownership, deployment target, dependencies).

## Decision

What did we choose? State it in one or two sentences, then add the details a
future contributor needs to apply it consistently.

## Alternatives considered

For each alternative, say why it lost. "It is worse" is not a reason; name the
constraint it violated or the cost it added.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what would have to change if this
decision is revisited? Mention the guard rails that keep the decision honest.

## References

Links to code, docs, issues or external material that support the decision.
